---
title: "Choose who can open an app"
description: "Set an app to Only me, Anyone with the link or password protection in Settings & access, and see what visitors get for each choice."
---

Every app has an access setting. LiveHQ's edge enforces it before a request reaches your server, so a visitor who is not allowed in never touches your app.

A new app starts private. On the Deploy form the two choices are named **Private** and **Public**; **Private** is preselected. After the first deployment you change the setting on the app's page, where the same two choices are named **Only me** and **Anyone with the link**, and a third is added: a password.

## The choices

| Choice | Who gets in | What a visitor sees first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Only me** | You, signed in with your LiveHQ account. In a Teams workspace: its owners, and the member who created the app. | A LiveHQ page that asks for an email address and sends a sign-in link to people who have access |
| **Anyone with the link** | Everyone. No sign-in, no password. | Your app |
| **Password protection** | Anyone who has the app's password | A LiveHQ page that asks for the password, then your app |

These three choices are the same on every plan. The console has no control today for inviting one person to one app. See [Share a private app with people](/apps/sharing).

## Change the setting

Owners, admins and developers can change it. Viewers see the setting with its buttons switched off.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the card">
    Open the app, then **Settings & access**. Find the card **Who can open this app?**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a choice">
    Press **Only me** or **Anyone with the link**. There is no save button: the console answers "Access settings updated."

    For a password, press **Enable password protection** in the row below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password protection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the password">
    Press **Enable password protection**. LiveHQ creates the password; you cannot choose your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy it now">
    The password appears once, under **Copy before leaving this page**. Copy it, store it somewhere safe, then press **Hide password**. LiveHQ keeps only what it needs to check the password, not the password, so it cannot show it to you again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it to the people you choose">
    Send them the app's address and the password, in two separate messages if you can.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What a visitor does: they open the address and see a page titled "Welcome to storefront" with the line "Enter the password shared by the app owner." They type it under **App password** and press **Open app**. The app then stays open in that browser for 12 hours.

A password opens the app and nothing else. It creates no LiveHQ account and gives no way into the console, the server or the database.

- **Lost the password, or want to shut people out?** Press **Generate replacement password**. The old password stops working and everyone who used it is signed out.
- **Turn the password off.** Press **Only me** or **Anyone with the link**.

## When a change takes effect

Within seconds, at the edge. No redeployment and no restart.

- Changing the choice, or replacing the password, ends the visitor sessions opened under the old setting. Open connections such as WebSockets are closed.
- While an app is not public, its responses are marked private so shared caches do not keep them. When you move an app away from **Anyone with the link**, LiveHQ also asks for copies cached earlier to be cleared. That runs in the background and can take a few minutes.

## Open your own private app

Press **Open app** at the top of the app's page. The console signs you in on the way, so the app opens directly in a new tab.

If you type the address into a browser where you have no session, you get the sign-in page instead. Enter your LiveHQ email address and press **Email me a sign-in link**. The page answers "If that address has access, a sign-in link is on its way." whether or not it does.

Each address has its own session. Being signed in at `storefront.livehq.app` does not sign you in at `www.example.com`.

## What private access does not do

- **It is for browsers.** A script, a mobile app, a webhook or another server cannot pass **Only me** or a password. If another service must call your app, set it to **Anyone with the link** and check an API key or a signed token in your own code.
- **It is one setting for the whole app.** You cannot protect one path and leave the rest public. A firewall rule can block or challenge a path: see [Write firewall rules](/apps/firewall/rules).
- **Private apps pause when LiveHQ is down.** LiveHQ checks every visitor of a private app, so it cannot open during a LiveHQ outage. Public apps can keep serving for up to 72 hours. See [What happens during an outage](/help/outages).
- **Other sites cannot write to it.** On a private app, LiveHQ refuses form posts and WebSocket connections that the browser reports as coming from another site, or from another app on `livehq.app`. Serve the pages and the API they call from one app or one custom domain.

The [app firewall](/apps/firewall/overview) runs before the access check, so it protects the sign-in and password pages too.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your own app shows "Use the email address that has access." | You opened the address directly in a browser with no session for it. | Use **Open app** in the console, or ask for the sign-in link with your LiveHQ email address. |
| "You do not have access to this app." after **Open app** | The app is **Only me** and you are neither an owner of the workspace nor the member who created the app. | Ask an owner to open it, or to choose another access setting. |
| A visitor is asked for the password again | A session lasts 12 hours, or the password was replaced, or the setting changed. | They enter the current password. |
| "That password is not correct. Try again." | The password is wrong, or it was replaced. After 20 attempts in an hour from one address, counting password tries and sign-in link requests on any app together, LiveHQ refuses more until the hour has passed. | Check the password. If it is lost, press **Generate replacement password**. |
| No sign-in link arrives | The address has no access, the email is in spam, or three links were already sent to it in 15 minutes. | Check the address and the spam folder, then wait 15 minutes. |
| An API client receives a web page in place of your app's answer | The app is private. | See "It is for browsers" above. |
| "Allow pop-ups for LiveHQ to open your app in a new tab." | The browser blocked the tab **Open app** needs. | Allow pop-ups for the console. |
