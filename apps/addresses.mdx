---
title: "An app's LiveHQ address"
description: "How an app gets its livehq.app address, how to choose an exact one, and how to change it later in the Domains tab."
---

Every app gets an address on LiveHQ's domain, such as `storefront.livehq.app`. It works over HTTPS as soon as the first deployment is live, with nothing to set up, on every plan. You can also [use your own domain](/apps/custom-domains) beside it.

A [background service](/apps/background-services) has no address: nothing on the internet can reach it.

## How the address is chosen

When you deploy a new app, the Deploy form shows the address it will get, under the app's name.

- LiveHQ builds the name from the app's name: lower-case letters and digits are kept, everything else becomes a hyphen, and the result is cut at 40 characters. An app named `My Storefront` gets `my-storefront.livehq.app`.
- If that name is taken, or is on the reserved list below, LiveHQ adds a short suffix, such as `my-storefront-3fa9c1.livehq.app`.
- A name that leaves fewer than two usable characters becomes `app`.

### Ask for an exact address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the address field">
    On the Deploy form, next to the proposed address, press **Choose address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the name you want">
    Type the part before `.livehq.app`. The rules are in the table below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Press **Deploy application**. LiveHQ uses exactly that address. If someone already has it, the deployment stops so you can choose another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Use automatic address** goes back to the proposed name.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 2 to 40 characters |
| Characters | Lower-case letters, digits and single hyphens. No hyphen at the start or the end. |
| Reserved names | `www`, `api`, `auth`, `login`, `logout`, `admin`, `account`, `accounts`, `support`, `status`, `docs`, `download`, `downloads`, `releases`, `cdn`, `assets`, `mail`, `email`, `billing`, `livehq`, `localhost`, `preview`, and names starting with `xn--` |

## Change the address later

Owners, admins and developers can change it. No redeployment is needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Domains tab">
    Open the app, then **Domains**. The first card is **App address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the new name">
    Change **Subdomain**. The console warns: "Saving replaces storefront.livehq.app; links to the old address stop working."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Save address**. The console answers "Your address has been updated."
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The old address stops working when you save. Bookmarks, links in emails, webhook addresses at other services and sign-in callback addresses that use it all break. Update them, or keep the address.
</Warning>

For 30 days after the change, no other app can take the old name, and you can change back to it.

Your custom domains and the app's access setting are not affected by the change.

## Open the app and copy its address

At the top of the app's page:

- **Open app** opens the app in a new browser tab. If the app is private, the console signs you in on the way, so you do not see a sign-in page. When a custom domain is connected, **Open app** goes to the first connected domain.
- **Copy URL** copies the full address, such as `https://storefront.livehq.app/`.
- The header lists the LiveHQ address, then up to two connected custom domains, each with a small button that opens that address.

**Open app** appears once the app has a published release. Until then the header says "Your app is waiting for its first published release."

## HTTPS

LiveHQ's edge serves the address over HTTPS and looks after the certificate. Your app speaks plain HTTP on its port inside the server; it needs no certificate of its own.

## Who can open the address

The address is public on the internet, but who gets past it is your choice. A new app starts **Private** unless you chose **Public** on the Deploy form. See [Choose who can open an app](/apps/access).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Use 2–40 lowercase letters, numbers or single hyphens, without a leading or trailing hyphen." | The name breaks a rule in the table above. | Fix the name. |
| "This address is reserved. Choose another." | The name is on the reserved list. | Choose another name. |
| "This address is already taken. Your current address has not changed." | Another app has it, or had it in the last 30 days. | Choose another name. |
| "Allow pop-ups for LiveHQ to open your app in a new tab." | Your browser blocked the new tab. | Allow pop-ups for the console's address, then press **Open app** again. |
| A page that says "There's no app at this address" | The address was changed or the app was deleted. | Check the app's current address in its header. |
| A page that says "This app is offline" | The server that runs the app is not connected to LiveHQ. | See [The app is unavailable](/help/app-unavailable). |

## Limits

- One LiveHQ address per app. For more names, add [custom domains](/apps/custom-domains).
- The part after the name is always LiveHQ's domain. You cannot nest names such as `shop.acme.livehq.app`.
- Browsers treat every `livehq.app` app as part of one site. For your app's own session cookies, use names with the `__Host-` prefix and do not set a cookie `Domain`. LiveHQ removes the `Domain` attribute from cookies your app sets on a `livehq.app` address, so a cookie never reaches another customer's app.
