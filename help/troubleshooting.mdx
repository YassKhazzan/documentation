Find what you see in the left column and follow the link. Each page says what the message means and what to try, in order.

## Start here: read what LiveHQ already says

LiveHQ keeps the reason for most failures in plain words. Before anything else:

1. Open **Home**. **Needs attention** lists failed deployments and offline servers, often with a one-click fix.
2. Open the app. The banner at the top of **Overview** says what state it is in and why.
3. Open the app's **Deployments** tab and the latest deployment. The build output ends with the reason.
